Caught Up & Complete Series

I defined dynamic content modules that allowed for the page structure to evolve based on a fan's progress through the series.

Anime is unique in that a single season can move through distinct arcs and major events, so rather than one static layout, the information architecture itself shifts to match where the fan and the show currently stand. The four layouts below show that evolution: before starting, midway through, between seasons, and after completing the series.

Key Considerations

Beauty & Clarity

Make the page beautiful and compelling, so the show is presented at its best and the watch/don't-watch decision is easy.

Usability

Improve organization and focus: episode selection, information hierarchy, action hierarchy, and reducing overall clutter.

Desktop Web Introduction UI

Platform Divergence

Question how the page should differ across platforms. Does a one-size-fits-all responsive layout serve desktop, tablet, and mobile equally well, or does it need to diverge?

The Flywheel

Incorporate more of the broader fan flywheel, a holistic experience that's additive to streaming and actively grows fandom, rather than sitting apart from it.

Living Room Shop UI

Lifecycle Awareness

Design for where both the user and the show are in their lifecycle: a brand-new viewer discovering a completed series needs something different than a long-time fan following a simulcast week to week.

Desktop Web UI - Upon Completion of a Series
